# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Be kind, respectful, and considerate. Avoid harassment, discrimination, and offensive behavior.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers may issue warnings or take appropriate action when these standards are violated.
